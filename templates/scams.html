Beware of Recent Cyber Scams in India

1. Fake "Digital Arrest" Video Call Scam 👇

What Happens: A person in a police uniform calls you on Skype or WhatsApp claiming a courier package in your name was seized with illegal items. They intimidate you on video call and demand money for "verification."

The Truth: Police, CBI, or Customs NEVER conduct trials or make arrests over video calls, nor do they ask for money online.

💡 Why it works: Scammers use high-pressure tactics and fake backdrops resembling police stations to create panic.

🚨 Action: Disconnect immediately. Dial 1930 or go to your nearest police station.

2. Fake Electricity / Ration Cut-Off Alert 👇

What Happens: An urgent SMS claims your power connection will be cut off tonight due to an unpaid bill and gives a personal mobile number to call.

The Truth: Electricity departments issue formal notice letters; they never cut power within hours via SMS threats.

💡 Why it works: Threatening service disconnection late at night forces people to act before checking facts.

🚨 Action: Never call the number in the SMS. Check your printed physical bill or official website.

3. "Like YouTube Videos & Earn ₹2,000" Scam 👇

What Happens: Messages offer easy income for liking videos. They pay ₹100 initially to earn trust, then ask you to pay a "deposit" to unlock bigger tasks before blocking you.

The Truth: Real jobs never ask you to pay money first to earn money.

💡 Why it works: Small initial payouts trick targets into believing the system is fully legitimate and safe.

🚨 Action: Ignore work offers that demand advance fees or deposits.
