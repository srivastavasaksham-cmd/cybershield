Everyday Safety Steps (Self-Defense Rules)

Click on any rule card below to read deep practical safety habits.

1. The UPI Locker Rule (House Key Analogy)

+
💡 Analogy: When someone gives you cash in hand, do you give them your locker key? No!

Your UPI PIN is ONLY used to SEND money out or check your balance. You NEVER enter a PIN to receive cashback or rewards.

🛡️ Golden Habit:

If someone sends you a QR code claiming "Scan this to collect ₹1,000 relief reward," it is 100% a scam. Scanning always deducts money!

2. Secret Signature Rule (Protecting OTPs)

+
💡 Analogy: Giving your OTP to a caller is like signing a blank cheque for a stranger.

Bank managers, police officers, or gas company agents will NEVER ask for your 4-digit or 6-digit OTP code over phone calls.

🛡️ Golden Habit:

Never read out codes sent to your SMS inbox. Treat your OTP like your ATM debit card secret password!

Click card to expand details 👆

3. Beware of Mystery WhatsApp Files (.APK Files)

+
💡 Analogy: Installing a mystery .apk is like letting a stranger move into your house without checking their ID.

Never tap install on files ending in .apk sent over WhatsApp/SMS claiming to be "Electricity Bill Update" or "PM Subsidy Form".

🛡️ Golden Habit:

Only install mobile applications from the official Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Unknown APKs silently steal bank OTPs!

Click card to expand details 👆

4. Lock WhatsApp with Two-Step Verification

+
💡 Analogy: Double locking your front door so nobody can copy your house key.

Open WhatsApp → Settings → Account → Two-Step Verification → Turn On.

🛡️ Golden Habit:

Setting a 6-digit PIN prevents hackers from cloning your WhatsApp account on another phone even if they get your SMS OTP!

Recent Cyber Frauds in India

Real-world scam methods targeting citizens today. Learn to spot the signs!

1. Fake "Digital Arrest" & Police Video Call Scam

+

What happens: A fake caller in uniform video-calls you on WhatsApp claiming a courier in your name contained illegal items (drugs, fake passports) and demands money online to clear your arrest.

💡 Real Truth:

Indian law enforcement agencies (CBI, Customs, Police) NEVER conduct trials, settlements, or arrests on WhatsApp video calls, nor do they demand money to close cases!

🚨 Action Step: Disconnect immediately. Dial 1930 or visit your local police station.

2. Fake Electricity & Water Cut-Off SMS Alert

+

What happens: An urgent SMS arrives stating *"Your electricity will be cut off at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bill. Contact officer at 98xxxxxxx immediately."*

💡 Real Truth:

State electricity boards send formal written notices or updates. They NEVER cut off connections within hours based on a text from a personal mobile number!

🚨 Action Step: Never call the number in the SMS. Pay through your official electricity board counter or app.

3. Part-Time Telegram Task & YouTube Review Fraud

+

What happens: You receive an offer for work-from-home tasks (rating hotels or liking videos). They pay ₹200 initially, then demand a ₹2,000 "pre-investment deposit" for higher payouts, eventually locking your account.

💡 Real Truth:

Legitimate companies will never require job candidates to pay money upfront to get paid.

🚨 Action Step: Never transfer money to unlock job tasks. Report and block the sender.

Cyber Terminology Decoder

Complex technical terms explained in simple, practical everyday words.

📌 What is an APK File?

An .apk is an Android application installation package. Downloading APKs sent over messaging apps can install spyware that secretly reads bank SMS OTPs.

📌 What is an OTP?

One-Time Password is a unique 4 or 6-digit temporary security code sent by banks to verify high-value transactions. Sharing your OTP gives full access to your funds.

📌 What is Digital Arrest?

A psychological extortion scam where fraudsters video call targets pretending to be police officers, threatening immediate imprisonment unless money is wired.

📌 What is Phishing?

Fake SMS or emails containing malicious links designed to look identical to real bank websites in order to steal passwords and login details.
