📌 Mission & Core Purpose

📌 मिशन और मुख्य उद्देश्य

Cybershield is an educational and threat detection initiative designed to bridge the awareness gap among non-technical internet users in India. With rapid digital adoption across UPI payments, e-governance services, and social messaging apps, cybercriminals increasingly exploit psychological manipulation rather than technical system hacks.

साइबर शील्ड एक शैक्षणिक और खतरा पहचान पहल है जिसका उद्देश्य भारत में गैर-तकनीकी इंटरनेट उपयोगकर्ताओं के बीच जागरूकता अंतर को समाप्त करना है। यूपीआई भुगतानों और सोशल मैसेजिंग ऐप्स के तेजी से अपनाने के साथ, अपराधी अब मनोवैज्ञानिक हेरफेर का सहारा ले रहे हैं।

Everyday Safety Steps (Self-Defense Rules)

दैनिक सुरक्षा उपाय (आत्मरक्षण नियम)

Click on any rule card below to expand deep practical habits and real-world analogies.

विस्तृत व्यावहारिक आदतों को पढ़ने के लिए नीचे दिए गए कार्ड पर क्लिक करें।

1. The UPI Locker Rule (House Key Analogy)

1. यूपीआई लॉकर नियम

+
💡 Analogy: When someone gives you cash in hand, do you give them your locker key? No!

Your UPI PIN is ONLY used to SEND money out or check your balance. You NEVER enter a PIN to receive cashback or rewards.

आपका यूपीआई पिन केवल पैसे भेजने के लिए होता है। कैशबैक या इनाम प्राप्त करने के लिए कभी पिन न दर्ज करें।

🛡️ Golden Habit:

If someone sends you a QR code claiming "Scan this to collect ₹1,000 relief reward," it is 100% a scam. Scanning always deducts money!

2. Secret Signature Rule (Protecting OTPs)

2. गुप्त हस्ताक्षर नियम (OTP सुरक्षा)

+
💡 Analogy: Giving your OTP to a caller is like signing a blank cheque for a stranger.

Bank managers, police officers, or gas company agents will NEVER ask for your 4-digit or 6-digit OTP code over phone calls.

बैंक अधिकारी, पुलिस या गैस कंपनी एजेंट कभी भी फोन पर आपसे ओटीपी नहीं मांगते हैं।

🛡️ Golden Habit:

Never read out codes sent to your SMS inbox. Treat your OTP like your ATM debit card secret password!

3. Beware of Mystery WhatsApp Files (.APK)

3. अज्ञात .APK फाइलों से सावधान रहें

+
💡 Analogy: Installing a mystery .apk is like letting a stranger move into your house without checking their ID.

Never tap install on files ending in .apk sent over WhatsApp/SMS claiming to be "Electricity Bill Update" or "PM Subsidy Form".

व्हाट्सएप पर भेजी गई किसी भी .apk फाइल को भूलकर भी इंस्टॉल न करें। यह स्पाइवेयर हो सकती है।

🛡️ Golden Habit:

Only install mobile applications from the official Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Unknown APKs silently steal bank OTPs!

4. Lock WhatsApp with Two-Step Verification

4. टू-स्टेप वेरिफिकेशन से सुरक्षा

+
💡 Analogy: Double locking your front door so nobody can copy your house key.

Open WhatsApp → Settings → Account → Two-Step Verification → Turn On.

व्हाट्सएप सेटिंग्स → अकाउंट → टू-स्टेप वेरिफिकेशन में जाकर PIN ऑन करें।

🛡️ Golden Habit:

Setting a 6-digit PIN prevents hackers from cloning your WhatsApp account on another phone even if they intercept your SMS OTP!

Recent Cyber Frauds in India

भारत में हालिया साइबर धोखाधड़ी

Real-world scam techniques currently targeting citizens. Learn how they operate!

नागरिकों को निशाना बनाने वाले वास्तविक घोटाले। जानें कि ये कैसे काम करते हैं!

1. Fake "Digital Arrest" & Police Video Call Scam

What happens: Fraudsters video call target victims on WhatsApp wearing police uniforms, claiming an illegal parcel (drugs, forged passports) was intercepted in their name. They isolate victims on call for hours and demand money to "clear" the arrest.

🚨 Action Step: Disconnect video call immediately. Police and CBI NEVER hold trials or demand payments over WhatsApp video calls!

2. Fake Electricity & Water Disconnection SMS Scam

What happens: SMS alerts arrive claiming *"Electricity power cut scheduled at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bill. Call officer at 98xxxxxxx immediately."*

🚨 Action Step: Never call personal mobile numbers sent via SMS. Always pay through your official power utility app or website.

3. Telegram Part-Time Review & Investment Fraud

What happens: Offers arrive for work-from-home tasks (rating Google maps or YouTube videos). Initial payouts of ₹200 are sent to build trust, followed by demands for ₹5,000 "pre-investment deposits" for higher tiers.

🚨 Action Step: Legitimate companies never require job applicants to pay advance money to earn wages.

4. Courier Parcel / Customs Clearance Extortion

What happens: Automated calls state a FedEx or DHL package contains narcotics and has been seized at airport customs, threatening immediate imprisonment unless cleared via wire transfer.

🚨 Action Step: Customs officers notify citizens through formal written postal notices, never via phone threats.

Cyber Terminology Decoder

Complex technical terms explained in simple, practical everyday language.

📌 What is an .APK File?

An .apk is an Android application installation file. Unsigned APKs downloaded outside Google Play Store can contain hidden trojans that read bank SMS messages without user consent.

📌 What is OTP (One-Time Password)?

A temporary 4 or 6-digit passcode sent by banks to authenticate high-value transactions. Sharing your OTP gives attackers full access to your funds.

📌 What is Digital Arrest?

A psychological fraud technique where scammers impersonate law enforcement over video call, isolating victims through fear to extort online money transfers.

📌 What is Phishing?

Fake emails, messages, or websites designed to mirror legitimate platforms (like banks or government portals) to steal usernames and passwords.
