How to Keep Yourself Safe Every Day

1. The Golden UPI Rule: Your PIN is Your Locker Key 👇

💡 Everyday Example: When someone hands you cash, do you hand them your locker key? No!

Rule: You ONLY type your UPI PIN to send money or check balance. You NEVER need a PIN to receive money, rewards, or cashback. If a caller says "Enter PIN to collect ₹500," hang up immediately.

🛡️ Safety Habit: Always set daily transaction limits on your UPI app to protect your primary bank savings.

2. The "Secret Signature" Rule: Protect Your OTP 👇

💡 Everyday Example: Giving your OTP to someone is like signing a blank bank cheque for them.

Rule: Bank managers, police, gas agency staff, or electricity officers will NEVER call asking for your OTP. Never read out any code sent to your phone.

🛡️ Safety Habit: Real officials never ask for secret verification codes over phone calls.

3. Beware of Mystery WhatsApp Files (.APK Files) 👇

💡 Everyday Example: Don't let an unverified stranger enter your house without checking their ID.

Rule: Scammers send files disguised as "Electricity Bill Update" or "Ration Card List" ending in .apk. Installing these lets strangers read your private bank SMS remotely. Only install apps from the Google Play Store.

🛡️ Safety Habit: Check file extensions before opening any file received on messaging apps.

4. Lock Your WhatsApp with 2-Step Verification 👇

Rule: Open WhatsApp → Settings → Account → Two-Step Verification → Turn On. This creates a secret 6-digit PIN so no hacker can hijack your WhatsApp account onto another phone.

🛡️ Safety Habit: Add your email address during 2FA setup so you can easily recover your account if forgotten.
